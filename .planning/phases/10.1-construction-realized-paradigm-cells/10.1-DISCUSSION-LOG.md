### Dual-form handling (user-initiated correction)

**User's note:** Some languages use BOTH a morphological form AND a construction for the same cell (e.g. -ará suffix AND AUX:FUT + verb). The original "extend CellOverrideDialog" framing implied mutual exclusivity — the user corrected this.

**Decision:** Construction-realization is **additive**, not a replacement. Both a morphological rule and a construction rule can be active on the same cell; they compose.
